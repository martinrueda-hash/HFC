1 · Portfolio matrix

Market attractiveness vs. ability to win

Bubble size = market size. Scores run from 1 to 5; the quadrant split is at 3. Market size, market growth and market share are scored relative to the other countries in view, so a "5" means best of the portfolio. Click a bubble or a chip to find the country in the table below.

Scoring method and weights

Market size is scored on a log scale. Profitability, competitive intensity, brand strength, channel access and product fit are qualitative 1–5 scores from the data file (5 = most favourable for HFC). Weight changes apply immediately and are kept in this browser only.

Update the figures

Paste or load a CSV with one row per country and end market. The first line must be the header below; percentages are plain numbers (3.2 = 3.2 %). Imported data stays in this browser. To change the figures for everyone, edit data.js in the repository.
