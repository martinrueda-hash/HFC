      SAM and HFC data: Excel template
      1. Fill in the HFC market intelligence Excel template: paste Oxford Economics in Oxford Economics, your figures and 1–5 scores in Raw data, the year in Settings.
      2. Save it as .xlsx.
      3. Upload it here. The dashboard reads the Dashboard data sheet, which the template calculates for you.

      Uploaded data stays in this browser only. To change the figures for everyone, update data.js in the repository.

      CSV option

      Paste or load a CSV with one row per country and end market. The first line must be the header below; percentages are plain numbers (3.2 = 3.2%).
